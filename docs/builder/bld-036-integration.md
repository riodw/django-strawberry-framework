### The `_assert_denied(response)` helper — judged under `### The existence challenge`

Population re-derived, both trees:

```shell
$ git show HEAD:examples/fakeshop/test_query/test_products_api.py \
    | grep -c 'assert "Not authorized" in payload\["errors"\]\[0\]\["message"\]'    # 7
$ grep -c ... examples/fakeshop/test_query/test_products_api.py                     # 9
```

The repeated shape is three or four lines — `status_code == 200`, `payload.get("errors")`,
`payload["data"] is None`, then the message assertion — so a `_assert_denied(response)` helper would
collapse roughly 30 lines into 9 calls, and with 9 callers it is not a one-caller indirection. It would
clear the existence challenge on its own merits.

**Decision: recorded as deferred, not dispatched.** Two reasons, and the first is the decisive one:

1. **7 of the 9 sites are pre-existing at `HEAD`.** Consolidating them means a repair cycle rewriting
   seven assertions in code it did not otherwise touch, in a file that was clean at `HEAD` — the same
   argument Maintainer Decision B's additive-only mitigation makes for the dirty file, applied to the
   clean one. The cycle's own scope is two SKIPPED contracts and three weak pins; a 30-line assertion
   refactor is not in it, and widening a diff at the integration pass is exactly when a diff stops being
   reviewable against its plan.
2. **Three of the sites carry a site-specific comment explaining *why* the assertion has that shape** —
   that the denial raises on a non-null payload field so GraphQL nulls the whole `data`, which is the
   authorization-failure surface rather than an in-band `FieldError` entry, and at one site that this is
   "the security-ordering proof". That distinction is the contract those rows pin. A helper named
   `_assert_denied` hides it behind a name unless the comments move to the call sites, and if they move,
   the saving shrinks to the three mechanical lines. The consolidation is still worth doing; it wants to
   be done by a pass that can also decide where that explanation lives.

Routed to the deferred catalog for whichever pass next opens `test_products_api.py` for its own reasons.
No Worker 2 consolidation pass is requested.
